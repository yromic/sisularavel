## 1. Scope & Defect Governance

This register logs all architectural defects, ambiguity points, missing operational constraints, and inconsistencies identified during the audits of the **SISURAT Laravel Migration Contract Pack**, along with their verified resolution and traceability references.

Defect history is preserved; earlier findings are marked as resolved, superseded, or corrected rather than erased.
