# 24. DECISION LOG

## 1. Scope & Governance

This document maintains the official Architectural Decision Record (ADR) registry for the SISURAT migration to Laravel (initial implementation baseline: Laravel ^12.0 on PHP ^8.2 per `DEC-TECH-002`; future upgrade: Laravel 13).

Decisions are classified under one of the following statuses:
- **`APPROVED`:** Formally established by project specification, architectural mandate, or explicit stakeholder confirmation.
- **`SUPERSEDED`:** Replaced by a more granular or reconciled decision.
- **`DEFERRED`:** Evaluated and intentionally scheduled for future versions beyond v1.0.
- **`USER_DECISION_REQUIRED`:** A critical business or policy question requiring explicit user direction.
